## How I work

I use AI tooling every day, and I think that's worth stating plainly rather than hiding. The part that actually matters isn't prompting, it's reading the output. AI is confidently wrong often enough that accepting it unreviewed is how you ship bugs nobody can explain three weeks later. I read and rewrite most of what comes back, and I can explain every line I commit.

Most of my professional work has been inside existing team codebases rather than greenfield, which is where I've learned the most: changing something without breaking the workflows people already depend on.
